# Product Overview

**Nemuru (ねむる)** is a sleep and mental wellness Flutter app designed to help users process their emotions and thoughts before bedtime through AI-powered conversations.

## Core Features
- **Mood Check-in**: Users select their current emotional state (joy, anger, sadness, pleasure, fatigue, anxiety)
- **AI Conversations**: Interactive chat with 12 different AI characters, each with unique personalities and conversation styles
- **Chat History**: Local storage of conversation logs for reflection
- **Character Selection**: Multiple AI companions with distinct empathy styles, question approaches, and specialties
- **Subscription Model**: Free tier (4 characters, limited conversations) and premium tier (all characters, extended conversations)

## Target Audience
- Japanese-speaking users (app is primarily in Japanese)
- Adults seeking emotional support and better sleep habits
- Users looking for AI-powered mental wellness tools

## Key Design Principles
- **Privacy-first**: All personal data stored locally on device
- **Accessibility**: Font scaling, dark mode support
- **Simplicity**: Clean, intuitive interface focused on bedtime routine
- **Personalization**: Multiple AI characters to match user preferences

## Business Model
- Freemium app with in-app purchases
- Free version: 4 characters, 2 conversations/day, 5 turns per conversation
- Premium version: All 12 characters, 3 conversations/day, 30 turns per conversation
